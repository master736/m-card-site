M CARD PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective July 16, 2026

Our approach

M Card is designed to be guest-first and privacy-conscious. You can create and use a digital contact card without creating an M Card account.

Information and storage

Version 1.0 stores your current card information locally on your device. M Card does not collect, transmit, sell, or use that information for tracking or advertising.

Sharing

M Card shares information only when you choose an action such as displaying a QR code, opening a website, using your device’s share sheet, or creating a contact file. Information shared through another application is subject to that application’s privacy practices.

Photos and files

If you select an image for your card, the operating system provides access only to the item you choose. M Card uses it to display your card locally.

Data removal

You can remove locally stored M Card information by editing or clearing your card, clearing the app’s data, or uninstalling the app.

Children

M Card is a general business utility and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as M Card evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
